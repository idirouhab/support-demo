Art direction: a precise editorial operations tool for people who work in tickets all day. Understated, extremely composed, dense where useful, mature and bespoke. A matte graphite queue and a warm porcelain ticket canvas. Quiet contrast, typography and careful column rhythm carry the design. Fine rules, flat surfaces, small 3–4px control corners. A single pale citrine accent used sparingly. No gradients, glows, ornamental art, glass, 3D, drop shadows or rounded-card soup. No giant hero, no slogan, no avatar bubbles, no blue-purple SaaS kit.
Use a refined neutral sans similar to Neue Haas Grotesk / Geist; use a small crisp monospace ONLY for ticket IDs and utility metadata. Typography 13–15px for normal UI, 24–28px for the main ticket title, 11–12px for quiet section labels. Avoid heavy bold everywhere. Perfect legibility and strong contrast. Text is sharp, measured, professionally typeset.
